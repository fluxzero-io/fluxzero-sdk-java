## Where does Fluxzero shine?

Fluxzero can run products across domains and workload sizes, including SaaS products, marketplaces, portals, internal tools, consumer apps, and AI-enabled software. It is especially useful when:

- **You want your agent to focus on product features**: Your agent builds the behavior, rules, and user experience. Fluxzero provides and operates the technical foundation as your product evolves.
- **Your product needs room to grow**: Start with one application and give individual workloads more capacity as usage grows, while keeping the same product behavior.
- **Your product has complex rules**: Your agent expresses permissions, workflows, and business rules in the application. Fluxzero coordinates message delivery and state changes around them.
- **You need to understand what happened**: You and your agent can follow actions, data changes, and failures through the built-in audit trail and connected monitoring views. [Explore Product insight](/product-insight).

The same application model supports an early product and a high-throughput system with complex business logic. You can start small and scale without rebuilding the product on a different foundation. [See how Fluxzero carries that foundation](/how-it-works).
