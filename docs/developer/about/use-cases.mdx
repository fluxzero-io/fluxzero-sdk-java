## Where does Fluxzero shine?

Fluxzero can run products across domains and workload sizes, including SaaS products, marketplaces, portals, internal tools, consumer apps, and AI-enabled software. It is especially useful when:

- **You want to move from idea to product quickly**: Your coding agent can spend its context and tokens on product behavior instead of assembling infrastructure.
- **Scalable applications**: Fluxzero is designed to scale out of the box, making it ideal for applications that need to handle high loads or large datasets.
- **Complex business logic**: If your product has intricate business rules, Fluxzero's message-driven architecture keeps that behavior explicit without spreading it across technical layers.
- **Auditing and observability**: With built-in support for auditing and observability, Fluxzero is a great choice for applications that require detailed tracking of user actions and system events.

The same application model supports an early product and a high-throughput system with complex business logic. You can start small and scale without rebuilding the product on a different foundation. [See how Fluxzero carries that foundation](/how-it-works).
