**Fluxzero is the product cloud for you and your coding agent.** Describe what your product should do. Your agent builds its features, rules and user experience. Fluxzero provides and operates the foundation underneath: hosting, data, message delivery, security, recovery and observability.

You decide what a good result looks like, try the application and choose when to release it. Your agent can inspect the code, tests and recorded behavior to implement and check each change. Developers can work directly in the same code at any point.

For an effective way to build, [keep prompts about the product and work from the inside out](/docs/building/working-with-your-agent): logic and behavior tests, then integrations, then APIs and UI. The Fluxzero plugin provides the framework guidance, so one agent and a clear product idea are enough to start.

## Start with a working app

[Set up your agent](/docs/tools/agent-plugin), then try the Ticketing example. The first two tutorials take you through one local flow and one small change. You do not need a cloud subscription, payment account or coding experience to follow them.

## From local work to a live product

Local development runs on your computer. Devboard gives you a shared view of the app, tested scenarios and work in progress. Your agent can use the same environment to diagnose problems and check fixes.

<span id="3-connect-your-github-repository-to-fluxzero-cloud"></span>

Publishing delivers a checked version to Fluxzero, where your users can reach it without your computer running. The live application has its own data, configuration and access rules. Follow [Publishing your app](/docs/tutorials/cloud-deployment), then [publish updates](/docs/building/publish-updates) and [manage the live app](/docs/building/dashboard).

For the wider product story, see [How it works](/how-it-works), [Product code](/product-code) and [Product insight](/product-insight).
